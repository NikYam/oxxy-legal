Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 12, 2026

Tales & Towers does not collect, store on our servers or share any personal data.

What stays on your device

The game saves your progress, settings and a short log of finished battles (map, difficulty, result) in the app's own storage on your device. This data never leaves the device and is removed when you delete the app.

Network, accounts, ads

The game works offline. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no third-party tracking.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
