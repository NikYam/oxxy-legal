Tales & Towers – Support

Tales & Towers is a single-player tower defense game for iPhone, iPad and Mac with 30 maps from old folk tales.

Questions and feedback

Email nyamschikov@gmail.com – we usually reply within two days.

Progress and language

Progress is saved on your device automatically. The game follows your device language (English or Russian); you can switch it with the RU / EN button on the title screen.
