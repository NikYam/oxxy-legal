Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 22, 2026

This policy explains what information the TOEFL Prep iOS app (“TOEFL Prep”, “we”) collects, how it is used and the choices you have. TOEFL Prep is developed by Nikita Yamshchikov.

Information stored on your device

Your goal band, exam date, settings, answers, scores and voice recordings are stored locally on your iPhone. TOEFL Prep does not require an account and does not keep your answer history on our servers. Deleting the app deletes this data.

AI feedback

In version 1.0.2, when you allow AI feedback and submit a Speaking or Writing task, the app sends the recording or written answer and task instructions to our server on Cloudflare. Our server forwards the recording to OpenAI for transcription and sends the transcript or written answer with the task instructions to OpenAI for scoring. Earlier versions send this information directly to OpenAI. The app asks for permission before sending an answer. You can withdraw that permission in Settings under “Allow AI feedback”.

Cloudflare processes these requests on our behalf. Our server keeps no copy of recordings, transcripts, written answers or feedback. For scoring, the server requests that OpenAI not store the response. OpenAI processes requests under its API data controls. Reading, Listening and Build a Sentence tasks are scored on the device. OpenAI API data controls.

Daily limits

Starting with version 1.0.2, to enforce daily limits on AI feedback, our server keeps counters per day. Each counter is keyed by a salted one-way hash (not reversible to the original values) of a random install ID kept in your device’s Keychain, of your IP address and, for subscribers, of the original transaction ID of your App Store subscription. To confirm a subscription, the app sends the transaction signed by Apple to our server, which verifies it. The counters contain no names, email addresses or answer content.

Day counters are deleted automatically after 35 days. One more counter, which tracks the one-time free welcome reviews, is keyed only by the hashed install ID and is kept for as long as the service runs, so reinstalling the app does not reset it.

Microphone

The microphone is used only while you record an answer. Recordings stay on your iPhone unless you request AI feedback for them; then they are sent as described under “AI feedback”.

Analytics and tracking

Starting with version 1.0.2, TOEFL Prep sends usage events under a random installation identifier to understand which features help learners. The app sends these events to our own server, which runs on Cloudflare infrastructure. We do not share these events with analytics or advertising companies. Examples: the app or a screen was opened, onboarding finished, a lesson was studied, a practice task or test was started, completed or left, an AI review finished with a practice score or failed, the subscription screen was viewed or closed, a free trial started or a subscription was purchased or renewed (product, price and currency, never your payment details).

Each event carries a random identifier created on your device when the app is first opened, plus the app version, iOS version, device language, the country or region set on your device and a session number. We do not store your IP address with these events (the daily-limit counters described above use only a one-way hash of it), and we do not send your name, email, Apple Account, advertising identifier, precise location, the text or recordings of your answers, or the content of AI feedback. We do not combine these events with data from other companies’ apps or websites, do not use them for advertising and do not track you across apps and websites. We do not sell your personal information.

We keep analytics events for up to 24 months, then delete them. Deleting the app removes the random identifier from your device, so new events can no longer be linked to earlier ones. To ask us to delete analytics data already collected, write to the email address below; because we do not know who you are, please include the anonymous ID shown in Settings. You can also turn off “Share anonymous usage data” in Settings at any time; the app discards queued events and stops sending activity. It may send a final notification recording this choice.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. We never receive your payment details.

Children

TOEFL Prep is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows when it was last changed.
