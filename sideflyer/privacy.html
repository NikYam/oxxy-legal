Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 14, 2026

Side Flyer does not collect, store on our servers or share any personal data.

What stays on your device

The game saves your progress, unlocked planes and settings in the app's own storage on your device. This data never leaves the device and is removed when you delete the app.

Network, accounts, ads

The game works offline. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no third-party tracking.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
