Side Flyer – Support

Side Flyer is a side-view biplane game for iPhone, iPad and Mac: ten flights across an archipelago, a rival to outrun, the Black Aces to shoot down and a pier to land on.

Questions and feedback

Email nyamschikov@gmail.com – we usually reply within two days.

Controls

On iPhone and iPad, drag a finger on the left half of the screen to pull the nose up or push it down; the GAS, BRAKE, BOOST, FIRE, ROCKET and CANNON buttons are on the right. The game also supports gamepads, and on Mac the keyboard.

Progress and language

Progress is saved on your device automatically. The game follows your device language (English or Russian).
