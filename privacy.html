Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 4, 2026

This policy explains what information the Oxxy iOS app (“Oxxy”, “we”) collects, how it is used and the choices you have. Oxxy is developed by Nikita Yamshchikov.

Information stored on your device

Your practice history – completed exercises, minutes, breath count, level, awards and settings – is stored locally on your iPhone. Oxxy does not require an account and does not upload this history to our servers. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Analytics and crash reports

To understand which features are used and to fix crashes, Oxxy uses the following third-party services, which may receive app usage events (for example, “exercise finished”), device and app information (device model, OS version, app version, language), an app-specific device identifier and approximate location derived from your IP address:

  • Amplitude – product analytics (privacy policy)
  • Google Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics – analytics and crash reports (privacy information)
  • AppMetrica by Yandex – analytics and crash reports (privacy policy)
  • Meta (Facebook SDK) – app events for measuring app installs and usage (privacy policy)

Oxxy does not request permission to track you across apps and websites owned by other companies (App Tracking Transparency) and does not use the advertising identifier. We do not sell your personal information.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment details; the app only learns whether your subscription is active.

Contacting us

If you email us, we use your email address and message only to reply to you.

Children

Oxxy is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices and rights

You can stop all data collection by deleting the app. To ask about, access or delete data held by the analytics providers listed above in connection with your device, contact us at nyamschikov@gmail.com and we will help with the request.

Health information

Oxxy is a wellness app, not a medical device, and does not provide medical advice. We do not collect health records.

Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows when it was last changed.
