Oxxy: Breathing Exercises

Oxxy is a breathing app with guided exercises for calm, sleep, focus and sport, plus stats that show how much you've breathed.

Contact support

Questions, bugs or ideas? Write to nyamschikov@gmail.com. We usually reply within 2 business days.

Subscriptions

Oxxy Premium is an auto-renewable subscription billed through your Apple ID. You can manage or cancel it anytime in Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your iPhone. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Restore purchases

Open Profile → Settings → Subscription and tap “Restore subscription”.

Your data

Your practice history is stored on your device. See the Privacy Policy for details.
